**Nick Ivers** - Nick is an ecologist with interests in multi-trophic species interactions, mostly between flowers, bees, and parasites. He received his PhD from the University of Texas at Austin and studied parasitism in bees using both landscape epidemiology and community ecology. Nick was a postdoc in the Lopez-Uribe and Schilder labs and currently in the lab of Dr. Shalene Jha at the University of Texas, Austin.

**Jules Cruz (Technician)** - Jules received their B.S. in Biology from Lynn University in 2022. Their previous undergraduate research investigated the effects of temperature on metabolic rate during development in alfalfa leafcutting bees. At Penn State University, Jules was co-advised by Drs. Margarita López-Uribe and Ruud Schilder at the Ecology program, after which they continued in the role of laboratory technician. Jules is about to start PhD studies at the University of Massachusetts Amherst and generally interested in how abiotic and biotic stressors interact with pollinator health.
